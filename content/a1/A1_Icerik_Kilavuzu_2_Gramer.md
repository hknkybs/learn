# Gramer (G01–G30)

## G01 Özne zamirleri — Ü1

**Kural:** İngilizcede cümlenin öznesi her zaman söylenir. Türkçede "Geliyorum" deriz, özne fiilin içindedir; İngilizcede "I am coming" deriz, "I" atlanamaz. Türkçedeki tek "o", İngilizcede üçe ayrılır: erkek için **he**, kadın için **she**, eşya ve hayvan için **it**.

| İngilizce | Türkçe | Not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | ben | Her zaman büyük harf |
| you | sen / siz | Tekil ve çoğul aynı |
| he | o (erkek) | Ali, my father |
| she | o (kadın) | Ayşe, my mother |
| it | o (eşya, hayvan) | the car, the cat |
| we | biz |  |
| they | onlar | İnsan, eşya, hayvan |

**Örnekler**

- I am Hakan. — Ben Hakan'ım.
- She is my sister. — O benim kız kardeşim.
- He is a teacher. — O bir öğretmen.
- It is a cat. — O bir kedi.
- They are my friends. — Onlar benim arkadaşlarım.

**Dikkat (TR03):** "My sister, he is a doctor" yanlıştır. Kadın için she kullanılır: *My sister is a doctor. She is very nice.*

**Alıştırma — Uygun zamiri yaz**

1. Ahmet → \_\_\_
2. Elif and I → \_\_\_
3. the book → \_\_\_
4. my parents → \_\_\_
5. Zeynep → \_\_\_

**Cevaplar:** 1 he · 2 we · 3 it · 4 they · 5 she

## G02 to be (am / is / are) — Ü1, Ü2

**Kural:** Türkçede "-im, -sin, -dir" ekleri İngilizcede ayrı bir kelimeyle söylenir: **am, is, are**. "Ben açım" = *I am hungry*. Fiili olmayan her cümlede to be gerekir; atlanamaz.

|  | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru | Kısa cevap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | I am (I'm) | I am not (I'm not) | Am I…? | Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. |
| he / she / it | is ('s) | is not (isn't) | Is he…? | Yes, he is. / No, he isn't. |
| you / we / they | are ('re) | are not (aren't) | Are you…? | Yes, we are. / No, we aren't. |

**Örnekler**

- She is a doctor. — O bir doktor.
- I'm not tired. — Yorgun değilim.
- Is he busy? — Yes, he is. — O meşgul mü? — Evet.
- Are you from Istanbul? — No, I'm not. I'm from Sivas.
- We are at home. — Evdeyiz.
- It's cold today. — Bugün hava soğuk.

**Soru kurma:** to be öznenin önüne geçer: *You are happy.* → *Are you happy?*

**Dikkat:** Kısa cevapta olumlu kısaltma yapılmaz: *Yes, I am.* ("Yes, I'm." yanlış). TR01: "I hungry" değil, *I am hungry*.

**Alıştırma**

1. My brother \_\_\_ a student.
2. You \_\_\_ late. (olumsuz)
3. \_\_\_ they at school?
4. I \_\_\_ 42 years old.
5. \_\_\_ it your phone? — Yes, it \_\_\_.
6. Bu cümleyi soru yap: *She is Turkish.*

**Cevaplar:** 1 is · 2 aren't · 3 Are · 4 am · 5 Is / is · 6 Is she Turkish?

## G03 İyelik sıfatları — Ü3

**Kural:** Türkçede iyelik ekle yapılır (kitabı**m**); İngilizcede ismin önüne bir kelime gelir: **my** book. İsim değişmez, tekil veya çoğul olabilir: *my book, my books*.

| Özne | İyelik | Örnek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | my | my car — arabam |
| you | your | your name — adın |
| he | his | his wife — (onun) karısı |
| she | her | her husband — (onun) kocası |
| it | its | its tail — (onun) kuyruğu |
| we | our | our house — evimiz |
| they | their | their children — çocukları |

**Örnekler**

- My name is Burcu. — Adım Burcu.
- What's your phone number? — Telefon numaran ne?
- This is Ali. His sister is a nurse.
- Our office is in Levent.
- The dog is in its bed.

**Dikkat:** his/her seçimi **sahibe** göre yapılır, eşyaya göre değil: *Ayşe and her brother* (sahip Ayşe). **its** ile **it's** farklıdır: its = onun, it's = it is.

**Alıştırma**

1. I have a cat. \_\_\_ cat is black.
2. Mr Smith is here. \_\_\_ office is upstairs.
3. We love \_\_\_ city.
4. Ayşe, is this \_\_\_ bag?
5. The children are with \_\_\_ teacher.

**Cevaplar:** 1 My · 2 His · 3 our · 4 your · 5 their

## G04 İyelik 's — Ü3

**Kural:** "Ali'nin arabası" = **Ali's car**. Sahibin sonuna **'s** eklenir, sahip önde gelir. Sonu -s ile biten çoğul isimlerde yalnız kesme işareti konur: *my parents' house*.

**Örnekler**

- Ali's car is red. — Ali'nin arabası kırmızı.
- What's your sister's name? — Kız kardeşinin adı ne?
- This is Eylül's room.
- My parents' house is in Sivas.
- The children's toys are on the floor. (children -s ile bitmez → 's)

**Dikkat:** *Ali's* iki anlama gelebilir: Ali'nin (iyelik) veya Ali is (kısaltma). *Ali's a doctor* = Ali is a doctor. Cümlede ardından isim geliyorsa iyeliktir.

**Alıştırma — 's ile yaz**

1. Burcu'nun telefonu → \_\_\_
2. annemin arabası → \_\_\_
3. öğretmenlerin odası → \_\_\_
4. çocukların okulu (children) → \_\_\_

**Cevaplar:** 1 Burcu's phone · 2 my mother's car · 3 the teachers' room · 4 the children's school

## G05 Nesne zamirleri — Ü7

**Kural:** Fiilden veya edattan sonra özne zamiri değil nesne zamiri gelir. Türkçedeki "beni, bana, benimle" hepsi **me**'dir.

| Özne | Nesne | Türkçe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | me | beni, bana |
| you | you | seni, sana |
| he | him | onu, ona (erkek) |
| she | her | onu, ona (kadın) |
| it | it | onu, ona (eşya) |
| we | us | bizi, bize |
| they | them | onları, onlara |

**Örnekler**

- Call me tomorrow. — Yarın beni ara.
- I love her. — Onu seviyorum.
- Can you help us? — Bize yardım edebilir misin?
- Where are my keys? I can't find them.
- Come with me. — Benimle gel.

**Dikkat:** "Help I" yanlıştır → *Help me*. Edattan sonra da nesne zamiri: *for him, with them, to us*.

**Alıştırma**

1. This is Mehmet. I know \_\_\_.
2. Elif is my friend. I like \_\_\_.
3. Please listen to \_\_\_. (biz)
4. These are my shoes. I love \_\_\_.
5. Is this for \_\_\_? (ben)

**Cevaplar:** 1 him · 2 her · 3 us · 4 them · 5 me

## G06 Article: a / an / the — Ü4

**Kural:** Türkçede "öğretmenim" deriz; İngilizcede tekil sayılabilir ismin önünde mutlaka bir article olur. **a**: sessiz harf *sesi* ile başlayan kelime (a book, a car). **an**: ünlü *sesi* ile başlayan kelime (an apple, an engineer). **the**: konuşanların ikisinin de bildiği belirli şey (the door = bu odadaki kapı).

**Ne zaman hangisi?**

- İlk kez söz ediyorsak a/an: *I have a cat.*
- İkinci kez veya belli olan the: *The cat is black.*
- Tek olan şeyler the: *the sun, the sea*.
- Meslekler a/an ile: *She is a nurse. I am an engineer.*

**Örnekler**

- I've got a brother and a sister.
- She eats an apple every day.
- He is an engineer. — O bir mühendis.
- Close the window, please.
- It's an hour. (h okunmaz, ses ünlü) / a university (ses /ju/, sessiz)

**Dikkat (TR04):** "I am engineer" yanlıştır → *I am an engineer*. Çoğul ve sayılamayan isimlerde a/an kullanılmaz: *books, water*.

**Alıştırma — a, an veya the**

1. I'm \_\_\_ teacher.
2. She has \_\_\_ orange.
3. Open \_\_\_ door, please.
4. It's \_\_\_ old house.
5. My father is \_\_\_ doctor. \_\_\_ hospital is near our house.

**Cevaplar:** 1 a · 2 an · 3 the · 4 an · 5 a / The

## G07 Çoğul isimler — Ü4

**Kural:** Çoğu isme **-s** eklenir. Türkçeden farklı olarak sayıdan sonra da çoğul eki gelir: "iki elma" = *two apple**s***.

| Kural | Tekil → Çoğul |
| --- | --- |
| Çoğu isim: + s | car → cars, book → books |
| -s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + es | bus → buses, box → boxes, watch → watches, tomato → tomatoes |
| sessiz harf + y: y → ies | city → cities, baby → babies |
| ünlü + y: + s | day → days, boy → boys |
| -f / -fe: → ves | knife → knives, wife → wives |
| Düzensiz | man → men, woman → women, child → children, person → people, foot → feet, tooth → teeth, mouse → mice |
| Değişmeyen | fish → fish, sheep → sheep |

**Örnekler**

- I have two children. — İki çocuğum var.
- There are three boxes on the table.
- Five people are in the room.
- My feet are cold.
- Istanbul and Ankara are big cities.

**Dikkat (TR05):** "two apple" yanlış, *two apples* doğru. *people* zaten çoğuldur: "peoples" demeyiz.

**Alıştırma — Çoğul yap**

1. bus → \_\_\_
2. family → \_\_\_
3. woman → \_\_\_
4. key → \_\_\_
5. tooth → \_\_\_
6. three \_\_\_ (dress)

**Cevaplar:** 1 buses · 2 families · 3 women · 4 keys · 5 teeth · 6 dresses

## G08 Gösterme sıfatları: this / that / these / those — Ü4, Ü14

**Kural:** Yakındaki için **this** (tekil) ve **these** (çoğul); uzaktaki için **that** (tekil) ve **those** (çoğul) kullanılır.

|  | Yakın (bu / bunlar) | Uzak (şu, o / şunlar) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tekil | this book | that car |
| Çoğul | these books | those cars |

**Örnekler**

- This is my brother. — Bu benim erkek kardeşim.
- Is that your car? — Şu senin araban mı?
- These shoes are new.
- Those people are my colleagues.
- How much is this T-shirt? — Bu tişört ne kadar?

**Dikkat:** these / those ile fiil de çoğul olur: *These are my keys.* ("These is" yanlış). Telefonda kendini tanıtırken *This is Hakan.* denir.

**Alıştırma**

1. (elimdeki kalem) \_\_\_ is my pen.
2. (uzaktaki ev) \_\_\_ house is very old.
3. (masadaki kitaplar) \_\_\_ books are interesting.
4. (karşıdaki insanlar) Who are \_\_\_ people?

**Cevaplar:** 1 This · 2 That · 3 These · 4 those

## G09 There is / There are — Ü5

**Kural:** Bir yerde bir şeyin "var" olduğunu söylemek için kullanılır. Tekil ve sayılamayan isimle **there is**, çoğul isimle **there are**. Yer bilgisi genellikle sona gelir.

|  | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru | Kısa cevap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tekil | There is (There's) a bed. | There isn't a TV. | Is there a lift? | Yes, there is. / No, there isn't. |
| Çoğul | There are two chairs. | There aren't any shops. | Are there any parks? | Yes, there are. / No, there aren't. |

**Örnekler**

- There is a bed in my room. — Odamda bir yatak var.
- There are two chairs in the kitchen.
- There isn't any milk in the fridge.
- Is there a bank near here? — Yakınlarda banka var mı?
- There are 25 students in my class.

**Dikkat (TR06):** Türkçedeki "var" iki şekilde çevrilir. Bir yerde var → *there is/are*. Birinin sahip olduğu → *have/has*. "In my room have a bed" yanlış; *There is a bed in my room.* / *I have a bed.* doğru.

**Alıştırma**

1. \_\_\_ a sofa in the living room.
2. \_\_\_ three bedrooms in our flat.
3. \_\_\_ any eggs? — No, \_\_\_.
4. Ters çevir: "Bahçede bir ağaç var."

**Cevaplar:** 1 There is · 2 There are · 3 Are there / there aren't · 4 There is a tree in the garden.

## G10 Sayılabilen / sayılamayan isimler, some / any — Ü6

**Kural:** Sayılabilen isimlerin çoğulu olur (an egg, two eggs). Sayılamayanların çoğulu ve a/an'ı olmaz (water, milk, bread, rice, money, information). **some** olumlu cümlede, **any** olumsuz ve soru cümlesinde "biraz / hiç / hiç ... var mı" anlamında kullanılır. Teklif ve ricada soru olsa da some kullanılır.

|  | Sayılabilen (çoğul) | Sayılamayan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Olumlu | There are some eggs. | There is some water. |
| Olumsuz | There aren't any eggs. | There isn't any water. |
| Soru | Are there any eggs? | Is there any water? |
| Teklif / rica | Would you like some cookies? | Can I have some tea? |

**Örnekler**

- I'd like some water, please.
- There isn't any bread.
- Are there any apples? — Yes, there are some in the bag.
- She drinks some milk every morning.
- Is there any sugar in my coffee?

**Dikkat (TR10):** "informations, furnitures, breads" yanlış. Sayılamayan isim sayılırken kap ile sayılır: *a glass of water, a bottle of milk, a piece of bread, two cups of tea*.

**Alıştırma**

1. There is \_\_\_ cheese in the fridge.
2. I haven't got \_\_\_ money.
3. Have you got \_\_\_ brothers?
4. Can I have \_\_\_ juice, please?
5. Doğru mu? *I need some informations.*

**Cevaplar:** 1 some · 2 any · 3 any · 4 some · 5 Yanlış → I need some information.

## G11 How much / How many — Ü6, Ü14

**Kural:** "Kaç tane" için **How many** + çoğul isim; "ne kadar" için **How much** + sayılamayan isim. Fiyat sorarken de **How much**: *How much is it?*

**Örnekler**

- How many brothers have you got? — Kaç erkek kardeşin var?
- How many people are there in your family?
- How much water do you drink? — Ne kadar su içersin?
- How much is this jacket? — It's 50 euros.
- How much are these shoes? — They're 90 dollars.

**Dikkat:** Tekil fiyat *How much is…?*, çoğul fiyat *How much are…?*. How many'den sonra isim her zaman çoğul: "How many child" yanlış → *How many children*.

**Alıştırma**

1. How \_\_\_ coffee do you drink a day?
2. How \_\_\_ rooms are there in your house?
3. How \_\_\_ is the ticket?
4. How \_\_\_ students are in the class?
5. How \_\_\_ sugar do you want?

**Cevaplar:** 1 much · 2 many · 3 much · 4 many · 5 much

## G12 have / have got (sahiplik) — Ü7

**Kural:** Sahip olmayı "-im var" anlatır. İki biçim de doğrudur: **have** (Amerikan ve İngiliz) ve **have got** (özellikle İngiliz, konuşma dili). 3. tekilde **has / has got**.

|  | have | have got |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Olumlu | I have a car. / She has a cat. | I've got a car. / She's got a cat. |
| Olumsuz | I don't have a car. / She doesn't have a cat. | I haven't got a car. / She hasn't got a cat. |
| Soru | Do you have a car? / Does she have a cat? | Have you got a car? / Has she got a cat? |
| Kısa cevap | Yes, I do. / No, she doesn't. | Yes, I have. / No, she hasn't. |

**Örnekler**

- I have a daughter. — Bir kızım var.
- She has got blue eyes. — Mavi gözleri var.
- Have you got a pen? — Yes, I have.
- He doesn't have a car.
- We've got a small garden.

**Dikkat:** İki biçimi karıştırma: "Do you have got" ve "I don't have got" yanlış. Present Simple konusu (G14) önce görülmediği için Ü7'de **have got** biçimi öne çıkar; *do you have* biçimi Ü9'da tekrar edilir.

**Alıştırma**

1. My sister \_\_\_ got long hair.
2. \_\_\_ you got a bike? — No, I \_\_\_.
3. They \_\_\_ (have, olumsuz) a dog.
4. Has he got a car? — Yes, he \_\_\_.

**Cevaplar:** 1 has · 2 Have / haven't · 3 don't have (veya haven't got) · 4 has

## G13 Present Simple olumlu ve 3. tekil -s — Ü8

**Kural:** Alışkanlıkları, rutinleri ve genel gerçekleri anlatır (Türkçedeki geniş zaman: "çalışırım"). Fiil yalın haliyle kullanılır; yalnız **he / she / it** ile fiile **-s** eklenir.

| Özne | Fiil |
| --- | --- |
| I / you / we / they | work, live, watch |
| he / she / it | works, lives, watches |

**3. tekil yazım kuralları**

| Kural | Örnek |
| --- | --- |
| Çoğu fiil: + s | work → works, play → plays |
| -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: + es | watch → watches, go → goes, do → does, wash → washes |
| sessiz harf + y: y → ies | study → studies, fly → flies |
| Düzensiz | have → has |

**Örnekler**

- I get up at 7 o'clock. — Saat 7'de kalkarım.
- She works in a bank. — Bankada çalışır.
- My father watches TV every evening.
- Eylül goes to school by bus.
- Water boils at 100 degrees.
- He studies English on Mondays.

**Dikkat (TR02):** "He work" yanlış → *He works*. Türkçede 3. tekil ek almaz ("o çalışır"), bu yüzden bu ek en sık unutulan ektir. Uygulama bu hatayı her ünitede tekrar sorar.

**Alıştırma — Fiili doğru biçimde yaz**

1. She (live) \_\_\_ in Ankara.
2. They (play) \_\_\_ football on Sundays.
3. My son (watch) \_\_\_ cartoons.
4. Burcu (study) \_\_\_ every night.
5. He (have) \_\_\_ breakfast at 8.
6. The shop (open) \_\_\_ at 9.

**Cevaplar:** 1 lives · 2 play · 3 watches · 4 studies · 5 has · 6 opens

## G14 Present Simple olumsuz ve soru (do / does) — Ü9

**Kural:** Olumsuz ve soru cümlesinde yardımcı fiil **do / does** kullanılır. **does** kullanıldığında ana fiil -s almaz; -s yardımcı fiile geçmiştir.

|  | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Olumsuz | I don't work. | He doesn't work. |
| Soru | Do you work? | Does he work? |
| Kısa cevap | Yes, I do. / No, I don't. | Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't. |
| Wh- soru | Where do you work? | Where does she work? |

**Örnekler**

- Does he work here? — No, he doesn't.
- I don't eat meat. — Et yemem.
- Do you like coffee? — Yes, I do.
- She doesn't drive. — Araba kullanmaz.
- What time does the film start?
- Where do your parents live?

**Dikkat (TR09):** Türkçede soru yalnız tonlama veya "-mi" ile yapılır. İngilizcede *Do/Does* gerekir: "You like tea?" yerine *Do you like tea?* Ayrıca "Does she works?" yanlış → *Does she work?*

**Alıştırma**

1. \_\_\_ you speak English? — Yes, I \_\_\_.
2. My brother \_\_\_ (not / like) fish.
3. \_\_\_ your wife work on Saturdays?
4. Where \_\_\_ you live?
5. Hatayı düzelt: *Does he plays tennis?*
6. Olumsuz yap: *We watch TV in the morning.*

**Cevaplar:** 1 Do / do · 2 doesn't like · 3 Does · 4 do · 5 Does he play tennis? · 6 We don't watch TV in the morning.

## G15 Sıklık zarfları ve cümledeki yeri — Ü10

**Kural:** Bir şeyi ne sıklıkla yaptığımızı anlatır. Zarf **ana fiilden önce**, ama **to be'den sonra** gelir.

| Zarf | Anlam | Sıklık |
| --- | --- | --- |
| always | her zaman | %100 |
| usually | genellikle | %80 |
| often | sık sık | %60 |
| sometimes | bazen | %40 |
| rarely | nadiren | %10 |
| never | asla, hiç | %0 |

**Yeri:** Özne + zarf + fiil → *I always get up at 7.* / Özne + to be + zarf → *She is never late.*

**Örnekler**

- I always get up at 7. — Her zaman 7'de kalkarım.
- He usually drinks tea in the morning.
- We sometimes go to the cinema.
- She is often tired after work.
- I never eat fast food.
- How often do you go to the gym? — Twice a week.

**Dikkat:** *never* zaten olumsuzdur; "I don't never" yanlış. "Ne sıklıkla?" sorusu: *How often…?* Cevapta *once / twice / three times a week* kullanılır.

**Alıştırma — Zarfı doğru yere koy**

1. I drink coffee. (usually)
2. He is happy. (always)
3. They watch TV. (never)
4. We are late. (sometimes)

**Cevaplar:** 1 I usually drink coffee. · 2 He is always happy. · 3 They never watch TV. · 4 We are sometimes late.

## G16 Soru kelimeleri — Ü2, Ü10

**Kural:** Soru kelimesi cümlenin başına gelir, ardından to be veya do/does gelir.

| Kelime | Anlam | Örnek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What | ne | What's your name? |
| Where | nerede, nereye | Where are you from? |
| When | ne zaman | When is your birthday? |
| Who | kim | Who is that man? |
| Why | neden | Why are you sad? — Because I'm tired. |
| How | nasıl | How are you? |
| Which | hangi (seçenekler arasında) | Which colour do you like, red or blue? |
| How old | kaç yaşında | How old are you? — I'm 42. |
| What time | saat kaçta | What time do you get up? |

**Kalıp:** Soru kelimesi + to be + özne → *Where is he?* / Soru kelimesi + do/does + özne + fiil → *Where does he work?*

**Dikkat:** Yaş to be ile söylenir: *I am 42.* ("I have 42 years" yanlış). Cevabı tam cümle olmayabilir: *Where are you from? — Turkey.*

**Alıştırma**

1. \_\_\_ is your birthday? — In May.
2. \_\_\_ is your teacher? — Mr Brown.
3. \_\_\_ do you live? — In Istanbul.
4. \_\_\_ are you? — I'm fine, thanks.
5. \_\_\_ is your son? — He's ten.
6. \_\_\_ do you study English? — Because I need it for my job.

**Cevaplar:** 1 When · 2 Who · 3 Where · 4 How · 5 How old · 6 Why

## G17 can / can't — Ü11

**Kural:** **can** üç iş yapar: yetenek (yüzebilirim), izin (oturabilir miyim?) ve rica (yardım eder misin?). Bütün öznelerle aynıdır ve ardından fiil yalın gelir.

| Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru | Kısa cevap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / she can swim. | I / she can't (cannot) swim. | Can you swim? | Yes, I can. / No, I can't. |

**Örnekler**

- I can speak Turkish and a little English. — Yetenek
- She can't drive. — Yetenek (olumsuz)
- Can I sit here? — İzin: Buraya oturabilir miyim?
- Can you help me, please? — Rica
- Can I have a coffee, please? — Sipariş / rica
- You can't park here. — Yasak

**Dikkat:** can'den sonra -s veya to gelmez: "She cans" ve "I can to swim" yanlış. Telaffuz (P07): olumlu *can* genelde zayıf /kən/, olumsuz *can't* vurgulu /kɑːnt/ veya /kænt/ okunur.

**Alıştırma**

1. My daughter \_\_\_ play the piano. (yetenek, olumlu)
2. I \_\_\_ swim. (yetenek, olumsuz)
3. \_\_\_ I open the window? (izin)
4. \_\_\_ you speak slowly, please? (rica)
5. Hatayı düzelt: *He can speaks German.*

**Cevaplar:** 1 can · 2 can't · 3 Can · 4 Can · 5 He can speak German.

## G18 Emir kipi ve let's — Ü12

**Kural:** Emir ve talimat için fiil yalın haliyle cümle başında kullanılır; özne yoktur. Olumsuz emirde **Don't** + fiil. Öneri için **Let's** + fiil (Hadi … -elim).

| Biçim | Örnek | Türkçe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Olumlu emir | Open the window. | Pencereyi aç. |
| Olumsuz emir | Don't be late. | Geç kalma. |
| Kibar emir | Sit down, please. | Lütfen oturun. |
| Öneri | Let's go. | Hadi gidelim. |
| Olumsuz öneri | Let's not wait. | Beklemeyelim. |

**Örnekler**

- Turn left at the bank. — Bankadan sola dön.
- Go straight on. — Düz git.
- Don't touch that! — Ona dokunma!
- Please call me tomorrow.
- Let's have lunch together.

**Dikkat:** Yol tarifi (F08) emir kipiyle verilir. Emir kipi sert gelebilir; ricada *please* veya *Can you…?* eklemek kibarlıktır.

**Alıştırma**

1. (sessiz ol) \_\_\_ quiet, please.
2. (koşma) \_\_\_ run in the corridor.
3. (hadi sinemaya gidelim) \_\_\_ to the cinema.
4. (sağa dön) \_\_\_ right.

**Cevaplar:** 1 Be · 2 Don't · 3 Let's go · 4 Turn

## G19 Present Continuous ve -ing yazım kuralları — Ü15

**Kural:** Şu anda olan eylemi anlatır (Türkçedeki "-yor" ile konuşma anında): **am / is / are + fiil-ing**.

|  | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | I'm cooking. | I'm not cooking. | Am I…? |
| he / she / it | She's cooking. | She isn't cooking. | Is she cooking? |
| you / we / they | They're cooking. | They aren't cooking. | Are they cooking? |

**-ing yazım kuralları**

| Kural | Örnek |
| --- | --- |
| Çoğu fiil: + ing | read → reading, cook → cooking |
| Sonu sessiz -e: e düşer | make → making, write → writing |
| Kısa fiil, sessiz + ünlü + sessiz: son harf ikilenir | run → running, sit → sitting, swim → swimming |
| -ie: y + ing | lie → lying, die → dying |

**Örnekler**

- She is cooking dinner now. — Şu an akşam yemeği pişiriyor.
- I'm reading a book.
- Look! It's raining.
- They aren't watching TV. They're sleeping.
- What are you doing? — I'm working.

**Dikkat:** to be atlanmaz: "She cooking" yanlış (TR01). Durum bildiren fiiller (like, love, know, want, have = sahip olmak) genelde -ing almaz: "I'm knowing" yanlış.

**Alıştırma**

1. Listen! The baby (cry) \_\_\_.
2. I (write) \_\_\_ an email.
3. They (not / swim) \_\_\_ now.
4. \_\_\_ you (listen) \_\_\_ to me?
5. -ing ekle: sit, dance, play, stop

**Cevaplar:** 1 is crying · 2 am writing · 3 aren't swimming · 4 Are / listening · 5 sitting, dancing, playing, stopping

## G20 Present Simple ve Present Continuous — Ü15

**Kural:** Alışkanlık, rutin ve genel gerçek → Present Simple. Şu anda olan → Present Continuous. Zaman ifadeleri ipucudur.

| Present Simple | Present Continuous |
| --- | --- |
| every day, usually, always, on Mondays | now, right now, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen! |
| I work every day. | I'm working now. |
| She usually drinks tea. | She's drinking coffee today. |

**Örnekler**

- I work in an office, but today I'm working at home.
- He plays tennis on Sundays. Now he's playing with his son.
- What do you do? — I'm a developer. (meslek)
- What are you doing? — I'm cooking. (şu anki eylem)

**Dikkat:** *What do you do?* mesleği sorar; *What are you doing?* şu an ne yaptığını sorar. Türkçede ikisi de "ne yapıyorsun?" olabilir.

**Alıştırma**

1. I (drink) \_\_\_ coffee every morning.
2. Shh! The children (sleep) \_\_\_.
3. She (not / work) \_\_\_ today. She's ill.
4. We usually (go) \_\_\_ to the park on Saturdays.
5. What \_\_\_ you \_\_\_? — I'm a teacher.

**Cevaplar:** 1 drink · 2 are sleeping · 3 isn't working · 4 go · 5 do / do

## G21 like / love / hate + -ing, would like — Ü13

**Kural:** Genel sevgi ve nefreti anlatmak için **like / love / hate + fiil-ing**. Kibar istek için **would like** (kısaltma *I'd like*) + isim veya *to* + fiil. *would like* = "istiyorum" un kibar biçimi; *like* = "severim".

| Anlam | Kalıp | Örnek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Genel olarak severim | like + -ing | I like reading. |
| Çok severim | love + -ing | She loves swimming. |
| Nefret ederim | hate + -ing | I hate getting up early. |
| Pek sevmem | don't like + -ing | He doesn't like cooking. |
| Şu an istiyorum (kibar) | would like + isim | I'd like a coffee, please. |
| Şu an yapmak istiyorum | would like to + fiil | I'd like to pay, please. |
| Teklif | Would you like…? | Would you like some tea? — Yes, please. / No, thank you. |

**Örnekler**

- I like reading. — Okumayı severim.
- My daughter loves dancing.
- Do you like cooking? — Not really.
- I'd like a coffee, please. — Bir kahve alabilir miyim?
- Would you like to come to dinner?

**Dikkat:** "Do you like a coffee?" teklif için yanlış → *Would you like a coffee?* "I like to read" de doğrudur ama A1'de -ing biçimi öğretilir.

**Alıştırma**

1. I love (travel) \_\_\_.
2. She \_\_\_ (would like) a glass of water.
3. \_\_\_ you like some cake? — Yes, please.
4. They hate (wait) \_\_\_.
5. Doğru seç: *I'd like / I like* a sandwich, please.

**Cevaplar:** 1 travelling (US: traveling) · 2 would like (She'd like) · 3 Would · 4 waiting · 5 I'd like

## G22 Yer edatları — Ü5, Ü12

**Kural:** Türkçede yer bir ekle (-de) veya isimden sonra gelen kelimeyle (masanın altında) söylenir. İngilizcede edat ismin **önüne** gelir: *under the table*.

| Edat | Anlam | Örnek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| in | içinde | in the box, in the kitchen |
| on | üstünde (temas) | on the table, on the wall |
| under | altında | under the bed |
| next to | yanında | next to the bank |
| behind | arkasında | behind the door |
| in front of | önünde | in front of the school |
| between | arasında | between the café and the bank |
| opposite | karşısında | opposite the park |

**Örnekler**

- The keys are on the table. — Anahtarlar masanın üstünde.
- The cat is under the bed.
- There's a picture on the wall.
- The pharmacy is next to the supermarket.
- The bank is between the post office and the café.

**Alıştırma**

## G23 Zaman edatları: at / on / in — Ü8

**Kural:** Türkçede hepsi "-de" iken İngilizcede üç farklı edat vardır. Küçükten büyüğe: **at** (saat), **on** (gün, tarih), **in** (ay, mevsim, yıl, günün bölümleri).

| at | on | in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| at 5 o'clock, at 7:30 | on Monday, on Sundays | in July, in summer |
| at night, at the weekend (UK) | on 5 May, on my birthday | in 2026 |
| at noon, at lunchtime | on Monday morning | in the morning / afternoon / evening |

**Örnekler**

- The meeting is at 5 o'clock. — Toplantı saat 5'te.
- I go to the gym on Mondays.
- My birthday is in July.
- I work in the morning and study at night.
- She was born in 1984. (Ü17 sonrası için)

**Dikkat (TR08):** "in Monday" yanlış → *on Monday*. *in the morning* ama *at night*. *every, next, last, today, tomorrow* önünde edat yoktur: *every day, tomorrow morning*.

**Alıştırma**

1. \_\_\_ Friday
2. \_\_\_ 8 o'clock
3. \_\_\_ December
4. \_\_\_ the evening
5. \_\_\_ night
6. \_\_\_ my birthday

**Cevaplar:** 1 on · 2 at · 3 in · 4 in · 5 at · 6 on

## G24 Sıfatlar: yeri ve çoğul almaması — Ü16

**Kural:** Sıfat ya ismin **önünde** (a big house) ya da **to be'den sonra** (The house is big) kullanılır. Sıfat hiçbir zaman çoğul eki almaz.

**Örnekler**

- It's a big house. / The house is big.
- She has got long hair.
- These are expensive shoes. ("expensives" yanlış)
- My car is old but my phone is new.
- It's a beautiful day.

**Sıra:** Birden fazla sıfatta genelde görüş → boyut → yaş → renk: *a nice big old red car*. A1'de en fazla iki sıfat kullanılır: *a small black bag*.

**Dikkat:** Sıfat ismin arkasına gelmez: "a house big" yanlış. Tek başına sıfat cümle olmaz: "Very big." yerine *It's very big.*

**Alıştırma**

1. Kelimeleri sırala: car / a / fast / is / it
2. Hatayı düzelt: *They are talls.*
3. Hatayı düzelt: *I have a dog small.*
4. Çevir: "Odam küçük ama güzel."

**Cevaplar:** 1 It is a fast car. · 2 They are tall. · 3 I have a small dog. · 4 My room is small but nice.

## G25 Bağlaçlar: and, but, or, because — Ü16

**Kural:** İki cümleyi veya kelimeyi bağlar.

| Bağlaç | Anlam | Örnek |
| --- | --- | --- |
| and | ve | I have a brother and a sister. |
| but | ama | The flat is small but nice. |
| or | veya, ya da | Tea or coffee? |
| because | çünkü | I'm tired because I work a lot. |

**Örnekler**

- I like pizza and pasta.
- He's rich but he isn't happy.
- Do you want to walk or take a taxi?
- I study English because I need it for work.
- She can't come because she's ill.

**Dikkat:** *because* "çünkü" demektir ve nedeni ondan sonra söyleriz. Türkçedeki "-dığı için" yapısı da *because* ile kurulur: "Yorgun olduğum için…" = *Because I'm tired, …* veya *… because I'm tired.* Olumsuz cümlede "ne … ne" için A1'de *or* kullanılır: *I don't drink tea or coffee.*

**Alıştırma**

1. I'm hungry \_\_\_ thirsty.
2. The car is old \_\_\_ it's fast.
3. Is it Monday \_\_\_ Tuesday today?
4. I can't sleep \_\_\_ it's very hot.
5. She likes cats \_\_\_ she doesn't like dogs.

**Cevaplar:** 1 and · 2 but · 3 or · 4 because · 5 but

## G26 Temel kelime sırası — Ü9

**Kural:** İngilizce cümle sırası sabittir: **Özne + Fiil + Nesne + Yer + Zaman**. Türkçede fiil sondadır (Ben her pazar parkta futbol oynarım); İngilizcede fiil özneden hemen sonra gelir.

| Özne | Fiil | Nesne | Yer | Zaman |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | play | football | in the park | on Sundays. |
| She | drinks | coffee | at work | every day. |
| We | watch | TV | at home | in the evening. |

**Örnekler**

- I play football in the park on Sundays.
- My father reads the newspaper every morning.
- They eat lunch in the canteen at 12.
- Burcu teaches English at a school.

**Dikkat (TR07):** "I every day coffee drink" yanlış → *I drink coffee every day.* Zaman ifadesi başa da gelebilir: *On Sundays, I play football in the park.*

**Alıştırma — Cümle kur (AT-04)**

1. tea / I / drink / in the morning
2. works / my sister / in a hospital
3. at home / we / dinner / eat / at 7
4. English / on Tuesdays / studies / he

## G27 very / really ile sıfat güçlendirme — Ü16

**Kural:** **very** ve **really** sıfattan önce gelir ve onu güçlendirir (çok, gerçekten). *really* konuşma dilinde daha sık ve daha duygusaldır.

**Örnekler**

- It's very cold today. — Bugün hava çok soğuk.
- The film is really good.
- She is very tall.
- This coffee is really nice.
- My job is not very interesting.

**Dikkat:** *very* fiili güçlendirmez: "I very like it" yanlış → *I really like it.* veya *I like it very much.* *not very* "pek … değil" anlamı verir: *It's not very expensive* = Pek pahalı değil.

**Alıştırma**

1. Hatayı düzelt: *I very love my city.*
2. Çevir: "Bu çanta gerçekten güzel."
3. Çevir: "Hava pek sıcak değil."

**Cevaplar:** 1 I really love my city. (veya I love my city very much.) · 2 This bag is really nice. · 3 It's not very hot.

## G28 Past Simple: was / were — Ü17 (A1+ köprü)

**Kural:** to be'nin geçmiş hali: **am / is → was**, **are → were**. "Dün evdeydim" = *I was at home yesterday*.

|  | Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru | Kısa cevap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / he / she / it | was | wasn't | Was he…? | Yes, he was. / No, he wasn't. |
| you / we / they | were | weren't | Were you…? | Yes, we were. / No, we weren't. |

**Geçmiş zaman ifadeleri:** yesterday, yesterday morning, last night, last week, last year, two days ago, in 2020.

**Örnekler**

- I was at home yesterday.
- We were in Sivas last summer.
- Was the film good? — Yes, it was.
- They weren't at work on Monday.
- Where were you last night? — I was at the cinema.
- I was born in 1984. — 1984'te doğdum.

**Dikkat:** "Doğdum" = *I was born* ("I born" yanlış). *ago* sayıdan sonra gelir: *two days ago* (iki gün önce).

**Alıştırma**

1. I \_\_\_ tired last night.
2. My parents \_\_\_ in Antalya last week.
3. \_\_\_ you at the meeting? — No, I \_\_\_.
4. The weather \_\_\_ (olumsuz) good yesterday.

**Cevaplar:** 1 was · 2 were · 3 Were / wasn't · 4 wasn't

## G29 Past Simple düzenli fiiller (giriş) — Ü17 (A1+ köprü)

**Kural:** Geçmişte bitmiş eylemler için düzenli fiillere **-ed** eklenir. Tüm öznelerle aynıdır. Olumsuz ve soruda **did / didn't** kullanılır; ana fiil yalın kalır.

| Yazım kuralı | Örnek |
| --- | --- |
| Çoğu fiil: + ed | watch → watched, work → worked, play → played |
| Sonu -e: + d | live → lived, like → liked |
| sessiz + y: y → ied | study → studied, try → tried |
| Kısa fiil, son harf ikilenir | stop → stopped, plan → planned |

| Olumlu | Olumsuz | Soru |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I watched TV. | I didn't watch TV. | Did you watch TV? — Yes, I did. |

**Okunuş (-ed):** /t/ worked, watched · /d/ played, lived · /ɪd/ wanted, needed (yalnız -t ve -d ile bitenlerde ek hece).

**Örnekler**

- I watched TV last night.
- She worked in Ankara in 2020.
- We visited my grandparents last weekend.
- Did you call your mother? — No, I didn't.
- He didn't like the film.

**Dikkat:** "Did you watched" yanlış → *Did you watch*. -ed'yi her zaman ayrı hece okuma: *worked* tek hecedir /wɜːkt/. Düzensiz fiiller (went, had, saw) A2'de öğretilir; en sık 10 tanesi tanıma düzeyinde verilir (S-04).

**Alıştırma**

1. I (cook) \_\_\_ dinner yesterday.
2. They (play) \_\_\_ tennis last Sunday.
3. She (study) \_\_\_ English last year.
4. \_\_\_ you (like) \_\_\_ the hotel?
5. We (not / watch) \_\_\_ the match.

**Cevaplar:** 1 cooked · 2 played · 3 studied · 4 Did / like · 5 didn't watch

## G30 Whose ve iyelik zamirleri — Ü17 (A1+ köprü)

**Kural:** "Kimin?" sorusu **Whose** ile sorulur. Cevapta ismi tekrar etmemek için iyelik zamiri kullanılır: *It's mine* (benimki).

| İyelik sıfatı (+ isim) | İyelik zamiri (tek başına) |
| --- | --- |
| my bag | mine |
| your bag | yours |
| his bag | his |
| her bag | hers |
| our bag | ours |
| their bag | theirs |

**Örnekler**

- Whose bag is this? — It's mine. — Bu kimin çantası? — Benim.
- Whose keys are these? — They're Ali's.
- Is this your phone? — No, it isn't mine. It's hers.
- This house is ours.

**Dikkat:** *Whose* (kimin) ile *Who's* (who is) aynı okunur ama farklıdır. İyelik zamirinden sonra isim gelmez: "mine bag" yanlış; kesme işareti de almaz: "your's" yanlış.

**Alıştırma**

1. \_\_\_ car is that? — It's my father's.
2. This is my pen. It's \_\_\_.
3. That is Ayşe's coat. It's \_\_\_.
4. These are our tickets. They're \_\_\_.
5. Is this \_\_\_ (senin) umbrella? — Yes, it's \_\_\_.

**Cevaplar:** 1 Whose · 2 mine · 3 hers · 4 ours · 5 your / mine
