# A1 Seviye Sonu Sınavı — Örnek Form

Sınav 5 bölüm ve en fazla 60 dakikadır; geçme koşulu toplam ≥ %70 ve her bölüm ≥ %60'tır (BR-17). Bu form, soru havuzunu oluşturacak editöre örnek ve zorluk referansıdır; uygulamada her deneme havuzdan farklı bir form üretir.

| Bölüm | Soru / görev | Süre | Ağırlık | Ölçtüğü |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A. Dil Kullanımı | 30 soru | 20 dk | %30 | G01–G30, TR01–TR10, kelime |
| B. Dinleme | 15 soru | 10 dk | %20 | F01–F12, sayı, saat, fiyat |
| C. Okuma | 15 soru | 12 dk | %20 | Kısa metin, ilan, form, mesaj |
| D. Yazma | 2 görev | 10 dk | %15 | Form doldurma, 30–50 kelimelik mesaj |
| E. Konuşma | 4 görev | 8 dk | %15 | Tanışma, tarif, rutin, rol yapma |

Konu dağılımı: A bölümünde her TR kodundan en az 1 soru, G28–G30'dan en fazla 3 soru (BR-05).

## A. Dil Kullanımı (örnek 30 soru)

1. \_\_\_ name is Elif. (a) She (b) Her (c) His
2. I \_\_\_ a student. (a) is (b) are (c) am
3. \_\_\_ he from Spain? (a) Is (b) Are (c) Does
4. This is \_\_\_ umbrella. (a) a (b) an (c) the
5. Two \_\_\_ are in the garden. (a) child (b) childs (c) children
6. \_\_\_ are my keys. (a) This (b) These (c) That
7. \_\_\_ a bank near here? (a) Is there (b) There is (c) Are there
8. We haven't got \_\_\_ milk. (a) some (b) any (c) a
9. How \_\_\_ apples do you want? (a) much (b) many (c) any
10. She \_\_\_ got two brothers. (a) have (b) has (c) is
11. My father \_\_\_ in an office. (a) work (b) works (c) working
12. \_\_\_ your sister like coffee? (a) Do (b) Is (c) Does
13. I \_\_\_ eat meat. (a) don't (b) doesn't (c) not
14. He is \_\_\_ late. (a) never (b) don't (c) not never
15. \_\_\_ do you live? — In Ankara. (a) What (b) Where (c) When
16. \_\_\_ you swim? — Yes, I can. (a) Do (b) Can (c) Are
17. \_\_\_ the door, please. (a) Close (b) Closes (c) Closing
18. Look! The children \_\_\_ football. (a) play (b) are playing (c) plays
19. I \_\_\_ to work every day. (a) am walking (b) walk (c) walks
20. I'd \_\_\_ a cup of tea, please. (a) like (b) likes (c) liking
21. She loves \_\_\_. (a) dance (b) dancing (c) dances
22. The cat is \_\_\_ the table. (a) under (b) at (c) of
23. My birthday is \_\_\_ June. (a) on (b) at (c) in
24. It's a \_\_\_. (a) car red (b) red car (c) reds car
25. I'm tired \_\_\_ I work a lot. (a) but (b) or (c) because
26. Doğru sıra: (a) I coffee drink every day. (b) I drink coffee every day. (c) I every day drink coffee.
27. The film is \_\_\_ good. (a) very (b) much (c) a lot
28. I \_\_\_ at home yesterday. (a) am (b) was (c) were
29. We \_\_\_ TV last night. (a) watch (b) watched (c) watching
30. Whose bag is this? — It's \_\_\_. (a) my (b) me (c) mine

**Cevap anahtarı A:** 1b 2c 3a 4b 5c 6b 7a 8b 9b 10b 11b 12c 13a 14a 15b 16b 17a 18b 19b 20a 21b 22a 23c 24b 25c 26b 27a 28b 29b 30c

## C. Okuma (örnek metin + 5 / 15 soru)

> Hi Sara,
>
> I'm in Izmir with my family. We're staying in a small hotel near the sea. The weather is hot and sunny. Every morning we have breakfast on the balcony, and then we go to the beach. My son loves swimming, but my daughter doesn't like the water. She likes reading on the beach. In the evening we walk in the city and eat fish. The restaurants here are very good and not expensive.
>
> See you next week!
>
> Deniz

1. Deniz is in Izmir with \_\_\_. (a) friends (b) his/her family (c) Sara
2. The hotel is \_\_\_. (a) big (b) near the sea (c) expensive
3. Doğru / Yanlış: Deniz's daughter loves swimming.
4. What do they do in the evening? (a) They swim. (b) They walk and eat fish. (c) They read.
5. The restaurants are \_\_\_. (a) cheap and good (b) expensive (c) closed

**Cevap anahtarı C:** 1b 2b 3 Yanlış 4b 5a

## D. Yazma

**Görev 1 — Form (5 puan):** Bir dil kursu kayıt formunu doldur: First name, Surname, Nationality, Job, Email (uydurma), Phone (uydurma), Why do you study English? (tek cümle). Her alan 1 puan; son alan *because* veya tam cümle içermeli.

**Görev 2 — Mesaj (10 puan):** Bir arkadaşına 30–50 kelimelik e-posta yaz: nerede yaşıyorsun, ailen, sıradan bir günün, hafta sonu ne yapmayı seviyorsun.

| Ölçüt | Puan | Otomatik kontrol (Faz 1) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Görev tamamlama (4 konu) | 4 | Anahtar kelime/yapı tespiti |
| Gramer doğruluğu (to be, -s, there is) | 3 | TR01, TR02, TR06 kontrolü |
| Kelime ve yazım | 2 | Sözlük kontrolü |
| Uzunluk 30–50 | 1 | Kelime sayısı |

## E. Konuşma

| Görev | Yönerge | Süre | Puanlama |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 Tanışma | Introduce yourself: name, age, city, job, family. | 45 sn | Telaffuz puanı + 5 bilgiden kaçı var |
| E2 Tarif | Look at the picture. What are the people doing? | 45 sn | En az 3 doğru Present Continuous cümlesi |
| E3 Rutin | Tell me about your typical day. | 60 sn | En az 4 Present Simple cümlesi + zaman ifadeleri (at 7, then, in the evening) |
| E4 Rol yapma | Kafede sipariş ver ve hesabı iste (uygulama garson). | 60 sn | Her replik ≥ 70 telaffuz puanı + doğru kalıp |

Konuşma bölümü puanı = %60 telaffuz puanı ortalaması + %40 içerik kontrolü (anahtar yapılar). Mikrofon izni yoksa bölüm "eksik" olur ve sertifika verilmez (BR-20).
